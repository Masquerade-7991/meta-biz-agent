---
title: Glossary
description: Plain definitions of the terms used in Helo.ai and WhatsApp, with links to where each is explained.
---

Short definitions, in alphabetical order.

**24-hour reply window.** After a customer messages you, you have 24 hours to reply freely. Each new customer message restarts the 24 hours. Outside it, you can only send approved templates. See [Inbox](/docs/guide/inbox) and [Broadcasts](/docs/guide/broadcasts).

**AI agent.** The assistant that answers customers on WhatsApp. Meta runs it; you set it up in Helo.ai. See [Build your AI agent](/docs/guide/build-agent).

**Broadcast.** One template message sent to many contacts at once. See [Broadcasts](/docs/guide/broadcasts).

**Business portfolio.** Meta's name for your business account, which owns your WhatsApp assets. In Helo.ai it is the business behind your workspace. See [Connect a WhatsApp number](/docs/guide/connect-number).

**Coexistence.** Using the same number in the WhatsApp Business app and with Helo.ai at the same time. See [Connect a WhatsApp number](/docs/guide/connect-number).

**Connection.** A link from your AI agent to one of your own systems, so it can look things up or take actions. See [Build your AI agent](/docs/guide/build-agent).

**Dummy mode.** A sample-data version of the console with no sign-in. Nothing in it reaches WhatsApp. See [Getting started](/docs/guide).

**Embedded Signup.** Meta's own screens where you connect your number and grant access to Helo.ai. See [Connect a WhatsApp number](/docs/guide/connect-number).

**Escalation.** An automatic alert when a ticket waits too long, sent to more people at each level. See [Tickets](/docs/guide/tickets) and [Settings](/docs/guide/settings).

**Handoff.** When a chat passes from the AI agent to your team, which opens a ticket. See [Inbox](/docs/guide/inbox).

**Knowledge.** What your AI agent knows about your business: your website, FAQs, documents and business details. See [Build your AI agent](/docs/guide/build-agent).

**Messaging limit.** How many different people you can start conversations with, with templates, in a day. It is set for your whole business portfolio. See [Broadcasts](/docs/guide/broadcasts).

**Opt-in.** A customer's agreement to receive messages from you. You need it before you message someone first. See [Contacts](/docs/guide/contacts) and [Broadcasts](/docs/guide/broadcasts).

**Phone number display name.** The business name customers see on WhatsApp. Meta reviews it. See [Connect a WhatsApp number](/docs/guide/connect-number).

**Quality rating.** Meta's green, yellow or red signal for how customers react to your messages. Low quality can lower your limit. See [Broadcasts](/docs/guide/broadcasts).

**Response target.** The time within which your team should first reply to, or resolve, a ticket. See [Tickets](/docs/guide/tickets) and [Settings](/docs/guide/settings).

**Rich reply.** A reply with buttons, lists or other visuals instead of plain text, which your agent can use. See [Build your AI agent](/docs/guide/build-agent).

**Roles.** Owner, admin, supervisor or agent: what each member of a workspace may do. See [Getting started](/docs/guide).

**Segment.** A saved group of contacts that match rules you set, for example a tag or a city. See [Contacts](/docs/guide/contacts).

**Skill.** A set of instructions that teaches your AI agent how to handle one kind of request. See [Build your AI agent](/docs/guide/build-agent).

**Template.** A message approved by Meta in advance, needed to message people outside the 24-hour reply window. Each is marketing, utility or authentication, and the category affects the price. See [Broadcasts](/docs/guide/broadcasts).

**Ticket.** One customer issue inside a chat, tracked until it is resolved. See [Tickets](/docs/guide/tickets).

**Two-step verification PIN.** The six-digit PIN set on a number when it is registered. Meta asks for it if the number is registered again. See [Connect a WhatsApp number](/docs/guide/connect-number).

**WhatsApp Business account.** The account in Meta that holds your numbers and templates. See [Connect a WhatsApp number](/docs/guide/connect-number).

**Workspace.** Your team's space in Helo.ai: its number, agents, chats, tickets, contacts and people. See [Getting started](/docs/guide).
