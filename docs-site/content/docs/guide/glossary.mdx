---
title: Glossary
description: Plain definitions of the terms used in Helo.ai and WhatsApp, with links to where each is explained.
---

Short definitions, in alphabetical order.

**24-hour reply window.** After a customer messages you, you have 24 hours to reply freely. Each new message from the customer restarts the 24 hours. Outside the window, you can only send approved templates. See [Inbox](/docs/guide/inbox).

**AI agent.** The assistant that answers customers on WhatsApp. Meta runs it, and you set it up in Helo.ai. See [Build your AI agent](/docs/guide/build-agent).

**Business portfolio.** Meta's name for your business account. It owns your WhatsApp assets, and Meta verifies your business here. In Helo.ai, it is the business behind your workspace. See [WhatsApp assets](/docs/guide/whatsapp-assets#what-your-whatsapp-assets-are).

**Coexistence.** Using the same number in the WhatsApp Business app and with Helo.ai at the same time. See [Connect a WhatsApp number](/docs/guide/connect-number#using-your-whatsapp-business-app-number).

**Connection.** A link from your AI agent to one of your own systems, so the agent can look things up or take actions. See [Connections](/docs/guide/build-agent/connections).

**Display name.** The business name customers see on WhatsApp. Meta reviews it. See [WhatsApp assets](/docs/guide/whatsapp-assets#display-name).

**Dummy mode.** A sample-data version of the console with no sign-in. Nothing in it reaches WhatsApp. See [Getting started](/docs/guide#try-it-without-a-number).

**Embedded Signup.** Meta's own screens, where you connect your number and give Helo.ai access. See [Connect a WhatsApp number](/docs/guide/connect-number#what-metas-signup-asks-you).

**Escalation.** An automatic alert when a ticket waits too long. Each level alerts more people. See [Tickets](/docs/guide/tickets).

**Handover.** When a chat passes from the AI agent to your team. A handover opens a ticket. See [Safety and handoff](/docs/guide/build-agent/safety-handoff).

**Knowledge.** What your AI agent knows about your business: your website, FAQs, documents and business details. See [Knowledge](/docs/guide/build-agent/knowledge).

**Messaging account.** The Meta account that holds your message templates and billing. Usually, each provider you work with gets its own. Together with the WhatsApp account, it used to be one account, called the WhatsApp Business account. See [WhatsApp assets](/docs/guide/whatsapp-assets#what-your-whatsapp-assets-are).

**Messaging limit.** How many different people you can message first in any 24 hours. All the numbers in your business portfolio share it. See [WhatsApp assets](/docs/guide/whatsapp-assets#messaging-limit).

**Opt-in.** A customer's agreement to receive messages from you. You need it before you message someone first. See [Contacts](/docs/guide/contacts).

**Quality rating.** Meta's green, yellow or red signal for how customers react to your messages. Low quality can lower your messaging limit. See [Message templates](/docs/guide/templates).

**Response target.** The time within which your team should first reply to, or resolve, a ticket. See [Tickets](/docs/guide/tickets).

**Rich reply.** A reply with buttons, a list or other visuals instead of plain text, which your AI agent can send. See [Abilities](/docs/guide/build-agent/abilities#rich-replies).

**Roles.** Owner, admin, supervisor or agent (a team member): what each member of a workspace may do. See [Getting started](/docs/guide#who-can-do-what).

**Segment.** A saved group of contacts that match rules you set, for example a tag or a city. See [Contacts](/docs/guide/contacts).

**Skill.** A set of instructions that teaches your AI agent how to handle one kind of request. See [Abilities](/docs/guide/build-agent/abilities#skills).

**Template.** A message that Meta approves in advance. You need one to message people outside the 24-hour reply window. Each template's category is marketing, utility or authentication, and the category affects the price. See [Message templates](/docs/guide/templates).

**Ticket.** One customer issue inside a chat, tracked until it is resolved. See [Tickets](/docs/guide/tickets).

**Two-step verification PIN.** The 6-digit PIN set on a number when it is registered. Meta asks for it if the number is registered again. See [WhatsApp assets](/docs/guide/whatsapp-assets#two-step-verification-pin).

**WhatsApp account.** The Meta account that holds one phone number and its business profile: your business's presence on WhatsApp. See [WhatsApp assets](/docs/guide/whatsapp-assets#what-your-whatsapp-assets-are).

**WhatsApp Business account.** Meta's former name for the account that held your numbers, templates and billing. Meta has split it into a WhatsApp account and a Messaging account. See [WhatsApp assets](/docs/guide/whatsapp-assets#what-your-whatsapp-assets-are).

**Workspace.** Your team's space in Helo.ai: its numbers, AI agents, chats, tickets, contacts and people. See [Getting started](/docs/guide#your-workspace).
