This page takes you from a new account to knowing where everything is. It also lists what to prepare before you connect a WhatsApp number.

## What Helo.ai is

Helo.ai is a console for running customer support on WhatsApp. It does 2 jobs:

- **Sets up your AI agent.** Meta runs the AI agent that answers customers on WhatsApp. Helo.ai is where you tell the agent who you are, what it knows and what it may do. See [Build your AI agent](/docs/guide/build-agent).
- **Runs the support desk.** Chats, tickets, contacts and reports for your team, in one place. When the agent hands a chat over, your team picks it up here.

Your WhatsApp Business account stays yours. It lives in Meta's tools, and you can always open it in WhatsApp Manager.

## Which WhatsApp you are connecting

Meta has 2 products, and they behave differently.

| | WhatsApp Business app | WhatsApp Business Platform |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | The app on your phone | The programmable service Helo.ai connects to |
| Best for | One person answering a few chats | Teams, automation and AI agents |
| Works with Helo.ai | Yes, as a number you keep using next to Helo.ai (coexistence) | Yes |

If your number is on the Business app today, you can keep the app and connect the same number to Helo.ai. Some app features stop working when you do. [Connect a WhatsApp number](/docs/guide/connect-number#using-your-whatsapp-business-app-number) lists them.

## Before you connect a number

Have these ready. The person connecting must be a workspace **owner**.

- **A Facebook login with admin access to your business on Meta.** Meta's signup asks for it.
- **A business portfolio** (Meta's name for your business account), or the details to create one during signup.
- **A phone number.** A new number must be able to receive a text message or a call to get a code, and it must not be on WhatsApp already. For the Business app route, you need the phone with the app on it.
- **A display name** for the number that matches your business. Meta reviews it. Generic words, slogans and personal names tend to be declined.
- **A way to pay Meta**, unless Helo.ai bills you for it. Meta charges for the messages you send, and you add a payment method in WhatsApp Manager.

<small>Source: [Meta — Business phone numbers](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/business-phone-numbers/phone-numbers), [Meta — Display names](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/display-names)</small>

Then follow [Connect a WhatsApp number](/docs/guide/connect-number). [WhatsApp assets](/docs/guide/whatsapp-assets) explains which numbers you can add and what Meta allows.

## Sign in

Open the console and enter your email. You can sign in with your password, or ask for a sign-in link by email. The link only confirms your email address; you finish setting up your account in the console.

## Your workspace

Everything in Helo.ai lives in a **workspace**: its WhatsApp numbers, AI agents, chats, tickets, contacts and team. You can belong to several workspaces. To switch between them, click the workspace name at the top of the sidebar.

### Invitations

Owners and admins invite people from **Settings › Members** (see [Settings](/docs/guide/settings)). When someone invites you to another workspace, the invitation shows up in the console. Accept it while you are signed in with the invited email. The emailed link alone doesn't add you.

## Finding your way around

The sidebar has 3 groups:

| Group | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| Work | Home, Inbox, Tickets, Contacts |
| AI agent | AI Agents, Analytics, Reports |
| Setup | Assets, Settings |

The shortcut <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>) jumps to any page, setting or quick action. The top bar also has search, a light and dark theme switch, help and notifications.

### Home

**Home** walks a new workspace through setup: connect a number, then build an agent. Once you've started, Home becomes your dashboard. It shows the last 7 days at a glance, quick links and the tickets that need you. Anything left unfinished shows as a note at the top.

## Who can do what

Every member has one role. Pages and buttons you can't use are hidden.

| Role | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything, including WhatsApp accounts, billing and other owners. |
| **Admin** | Runs the workspace: people, AI agents, settings and message templates. Can't change owners. |
| **Supervisor** | Leads the team: sees every chat, reassigns tickets, creates message templates and reads reports. |
| **Agent** (a team member) | Answers customers: chats, tickets and contacts. |

Only owners connect or disconnect WhatsApp numbers.

## Try it without a number

**Dummy mode** opens the whole console with sample data and no sign-in. It's a safe way to explore or to give a demo. Nothing in Dummy mode reaches WhatsApp.

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordions type="single">
<Accordion title="What is Meta Business Agent, and what does Helo.ai add?">
Meta Business Agent is Meta's AI agent for WhatsApp. Meta runs it and answers customers with it. Helo.ai is where you set the agent up, and it adds the support desk around it: the Inbox, tickets, contacts and reports for your team.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Do I need a Meta developer account?">
No. You need a Facebook login with admin access to your business on Meta. Meta's signup, which opens from the **Connect** button, does the rest. See [Before you connect a number](#before-you-connect-a-number).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I try the console without a real number?">
Yes. [Dummy mode](#try-it-without-a-number) shows the whole console with sample data, and nothing in it reaches WhatsApp.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Why can't I see some pages or buttons?">
Your role decides what you see, and anything you can't use is hidden. For example, **Assets** needs supervisor or above, and only owners connect numbers. See [Who can do what](#who-can-do-what).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What does Meta charge for?">
Meta charges for messages you send, not for messages customers send you. Replies from the AI agent cost about 4 to 5 US cents each, and each number gets 1,000 free service messages a month. See [Paying Meta](/docs/guide/whatsapp-assets#paying-meta).
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
