## Sign in

Open the console and enter your email. You can sign in with your password, or ask for a sign-in link by email. The link only confirms your email address. You finish setting up your account in the console.

## Your workspace

Everything in Helo.ai lives in a **workspace**: its WhatsApp number, AI agents, chats, tickets, contacts and team. You can belong to several workspaces. Switch between them from the workspace name at the top of the sidebar.

When someone invites you to another workspace, the invitation shows up in the console. Accept it while signed in with the invited email. The emailed link alone doesn't add you.

## Finding your way around

The sidebar has three groups:

| Group | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| Work | Home, Inbox, Tickets, Contacts |
| Automate & grow | AI Agents, Broadcasts, Analytics, Reports |
| Setup | WhatsApp, Settings |

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>) to jump to any page, setting or quick action.

**Home** walks a new workspace through setup: connect a number, then build an agent. Once you've started, Home becomes your dashboard. It shows the last 7 days at a glance, quick links, and the tickets that need you. Anything left unfinished shows as a note at the top.

## Who can do what

Every member has one role. Pages and buttons you can't use are hidden.

| Role | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything, including WhatsApp accounts, billing and other owners. |
| **Admin** | Runs the workspace: people, AI agents, settings and broadcasts. Can't change owners. |
| **Supervisor** | Leads the team: sees every chat, reassigns tickets, sends broadcasts and reads reports. |
| **Agent** | Answers customers: chats, tickets and contacts. |

Invite people from **Settings › Members**.

## Try it without a number

**Dummy mode** opens the whole console with sample data and no sign-in. It's a safe way to explore or give a demo. Nothing in dummy mode reaches WhatsApp.
