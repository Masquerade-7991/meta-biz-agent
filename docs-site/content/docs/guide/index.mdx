This page gets you from a new account to knowing where everything is, and what to prepare before you connect a WhatsApp number.

## What Helo.ai is

Helo.ai is a console for running customer support on WhatsApp. It does two jobs:

- **Sets up your AI agent.** The agent that answers customers on WhatsApp is run by Meta. Helo.ai is where you tell it who you are, what it knows and what it may do. See [Build your AI agent](/docs/guide/build-agent).
- **Runs the support desk.** Chats, tickets, contacts, broadcasts and reports for your human team, in one place. When the agent hands a chat over, your team picks it up here.

Your WhatsApp account stays yours. It lives in Meta's tools, and you can always open it in WhatsApp Manager.

## Which WhatsApp you are connecting

Meta has two products, and they behave differently.

| | WhatsApp Business app | WhatsApp Business Platform |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it is | The app on your phone | The programmable service Helo.ai connects to |
| Best for | One person answering a few chats | Teams, automation, AI agents, broadcasts |
| Works with Helo.ai | Yes, as a number you keep using next to Helo.ai (coexistence) | Yes |

If your number is on the Business app today, you can keep the app and connect the same number to Helo.ai. Some app features don't work alongside it. [Connect a WhatsApp number](/docs/guide/connect-number) lists them.

## Before you connect a number

Have these ready. The person connecting needs to be the workspace **owner**.

- **A Facebook login with admin access to your business on Meta.** Meta's signup asks for it.
- **A business portfolio** (Meta's name for your business account), or the details to create one during signup.
- **A phone number.** For a new number, it must be able to receive a text message or call to get a code, and must not already be used on WhatsApp. For the Business app route, you need the phone with the app on it.
- **A display name** for the number that matches your business. Meta reviews it. Names that are generic, slogans or personal names tend to be declined.
- **A way to pay Meta**, unless Helo.ai bills you for it. Meta charges for the messages you send, and you add a payment method in WhatsApp Manager.

<small>Source: [Meta — phone numbers](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/business-phone-numbers/phone-numbers), [Meta — display names](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/display-names)</small>

Then follow [Connect a WhatsApp number](/docs/guide/connect-number).

## Sign in

Open the console and enter your email. You can sign in with your password, or ask for a sign-in link by email. The link only confirms your email address. You finish setting up your account in the console.

## Your workspace

Everything in Helo.ai lives in a **workspace**: its WhatsApp number, AI agents, chats, tickets, contacts and team. You can belong to several workspaces. Switch between them from the workspace name at the top of the sidebar.

### Invitations

Owners and admins invite people from **Settings › Members** (see [Settings](/docs/guide/settings)). When someone invites you to another workspace, the invitation shows up in the console. Accept it while signed in with the invited email. The emailed link alone doesn't add you.

## Finding your way around

The sidebar has three groups:

| Group | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| Work | Home, Inbox, Tickets, Contacts |
| Automate & grow | AI Agents, Broadcasts, Analytics, Reports |
| Setup | WhatsApp, Settings |

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd> (or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>K</kbd>) to jump to any page, setting or quick action. The top bar also has search, a light and dark theme switch, help and notifications.

### Home

**Home** walks a new workspace through setup: connect a number, then build an agent. Once you've started, Home becomes your dashboard. It shows the last 7 days at a glance, quick links and the tickets that need you. Anything left unfinished shows as a note at the top.

![Home dashboard with the last 7 days, quick links and tickets that need attention](/screens/home.webp)

## Who can do what

Every member has one role. Pages and buttons you can't use are hidden.

| Role | What they do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything, including WhatsApp accounts, billing and other owners. |
| **Admin** | Runs the workspace: people, AI agents, settings and broadcasts. Can't change owners. |
| **Supervisor** | Leads the team: sees every chat, reassigns tickets, sends broadcasts and reads reports. |
| **Agent** | Answers customers: chats, tickets and contacts. |

Only owners connect or disconnect WhatsApp numbers.

## Try it without a number

**Dummy mode** opens the whole console with sample data and no sign-in. It's a safe way to explore or give a demo. Nothing in dummy mode reaches WhatsApp.
