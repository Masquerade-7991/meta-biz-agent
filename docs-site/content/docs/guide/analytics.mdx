**Analytics** and **Reports** are for supervisors and above.

## Analytics tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Conversations, how they were handled, and the trend against the period before. |
| AI agent | How much the AI handled alone, and how often it handed over. |
| Human support | Tickets opened and resolved, first-reply times, resolving on time, satisfaction. |
| Consumption | WhatsApp message and Business Agent costs. |
| Broadcasts | Sent, delivered, read and replied. |
| Logs | Individual tickets, handovers and escalations. |

Filter by date range and by agent. Each figure compares with the period just before.

## What the numbers mean

- **Conversation:** a chat where the customer, the AI or your team sent at least one message in the period.
- **AI alone:** the AI answered and nobody from your team wrote.
- **With the team:** someone from your team wrote.
- **No reply:** neither the AI nor your team answered.
- **Handed over:** the AI passed the chat to your team.
- **Resolved on time:** resolved within its response target.
- **Satisfaction:** customers' ratings (Good, Okay or Bad) after a ticket is resolved.

WhatsApp spend comes from Meta for the whole account, one day at a time in UTC. That's why cost per resolved conversation only shows when you look at all agents together.

## Reports

Download any report as a CSV, print it, or have it emailed on a schedule. The reports are:

- Business review;
- Day by day;
- Agent performance;
- Team members;
- Teams;
- Consumption and cost;
- Ticket log;
- Handover log;
- Escalation log;
- Message charges;
- Agent usage by hour.
