Analytics shows how conversations were handled, how fast your team answers and what WhatsApp costs. Reports turn the same numbers into downloads and emails.

## Before you start

- **Role:** supervisors, admins and owners can open **Analytics** and **Reports**. Agents don't see them.
- **Some data needs time.** Delivery ticks, message prices and the AI agent's own usage arrive from Meta after the fact, so recent hours can look incomplete.

## Read the Analytics page

Open **Analytics** in the sidebar.

![The Analytics overview with filters, key figures and charts](/screens/analytics.webp)

Use the bar at the top to narrow what you see:

- **Period:** Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Last 90 days, This month or a custom range.
- **Agents:** all, or one or more. Each agent answers on its own WhatsApp number.
- **Team** and **Person:** these narrow the ticket figures only.

Every figure is compared with the period just before it, of the same length. Days follow your business hours' time zone, set in **Settings › Hours & targets**.

### The tabs

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | Conversations, how they were handled, satisfaction, first reply time, spend, and agents side by side. |
| AI agent | How much the agent finished alone, handovers per day, how tickets opened, questions it couldn't answer, and how often each tool worked. |
| Human support | Tickets opened and resolved, first reply and resolution times, escalations, when customers write, satisfaction, and teams and people. |
| Consumption | Spend per day, by message category, and the AI agent's usage. |
| Logs | The individual tickets, handovers and escalations behind the numbers. |

Some figures are links. Click one to open the matching list, for example the tickets behind "Resolved on time".

### What the numbers mean

- **Conversation:** a chat where the customer, the AI agent or your team sent at least one message in the period. System notes alone don't count.
- **Handled by AI alone:** the agent answered and nobody from your team wrote. Chats nobody answered don't count as handled.
- **With the team:** someone from your team wrote.
- **No reply:** neither the agent nor your team answered.
- **Handover:** the agent passed the chat to your team.
- **Handover rate:** handovers out of all conversations.
- **Tickets resolved:** tickets resolved in the period out of tickets opened in it.
- **Typical first reply:** the median. Half of first replies were faster.
- **Resolved on time:** tickets resolved within their resolution target.
- **Customer satisfaction:** Good answers, with Okay counting half, out of all answers to the feedback question.

Read **Handled by AI alone** and **Customer satisfaction** together: letting the agent handle more only helps while customers stay happy.

<Callout type="info">
**Questions the AI couldn't answer** (AI agent tab) lists what customers asked that the agent answered with its fallback or handed over. Add the top ones to your agent's knowledge. See [Build your AI agent](/docs/guide/build-agent).
</Callout>

## Consumption and cost

The **Consumption** tab adds up what messaging costs you.

| Figure | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- |
| WhatsApp messages | Meta's billing data for the whole WhatsApp account, one day at a time in UTC. |
| Meta Business Agent | Meta's usage report for your AI agent: billable replies, tokens and cost. |
| By message category | Marketing, utility and other categories, as Meta priced each delivered message. |

Two things follow from how Meta reports spend:

- **Spend is per account and per UTC day, not per number or per local day.** That is why cost per resolved conversation only shows when you look at all agents together. Filtering to one agent would divide the whole account's spend by a part of its conversations.
- **The AI agent's usage appears once the agent has a payment method in Meta.** Until then, the tab says "Meta reports usage once a payment method is added". That's a state, not an error.

Meta bills Meta Business Agent per token rather than per conversation. The charge covers both the agent's work and delivering the message.

<small>Source: [Meta — pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing), [Meta — non-template message pricing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing/non-template-messages)</small>

Meta's rates, and the dates they change, are on Meta's own rate card. They change only on the first day of January, April, July or October. **Settings › Billing** shows this month's total, an optional monthly budget and the AI agent's usage. A budget only alerts you (owners get a notification and an email at 80% and 100%); it never stops messages.

## Reports

Open **Reports** in the sidebar. The **Library** shows every report as a small card, in two groups:

- **Summaries:** the numbers for a period, with charts and a table. Read them on screen, or download them as CSV or PDF.
- **Records:** one row per conversation, ticket, rating or charge. Read them on screen, a page at a time, or download them as CSV.

To find a report, type its name in the search box at the top.

![The Reports library: summaries and records as cards](/screens/reports.webp)

| --- | --- | --- |

| AI agent performance | How much the AI handles on its own: handled by AI alone, handover rate, no reply, satisfaction and agent cost, with handover reasons, tool success and each agent side by side. | Date, Agents |

| Spend & usage | What WhatsApp messages and the AI agent cost: spend by message category, the agent's replies and tokens, and your monthly budget used. | Date, Agents |

### Records

### Filters

Click a card to open the report. The filters sit under its title: **Date**, **Agents**, **Team** and **Assigned to**. A report only shows the filters it uses, so reports about spend or conversations have just **Date** and **Agents**. The **Date** menu shows the exact dates and the time zone, which is your business hours' time zone from **Settings › Hours & targets**. **Clear filters** puts them back to the last 30 days, all agents.

Filters stay in the page address, so they carry over when you move between reports. On **Analytics**, **Open as report** opens the matching report with the same filters.

### Read a summary

![The Business review summary: tiles, charts and the table over time](/screens/report-summary.webp)

From top to bottom, a summary has:

- **Tiles** with the period's key numbers. Each one shows the change against the period just before it, of the same length, and most have a small line of the days. For rates, the change is in points (pts). Green means better: for reply times and costs, lower is better. Hover over ⓘ beside a tile's name to read what it counts (see [What the report numbers mean](#what-the-report-numbers-mean)).
- **Charts**, for example **Conversations by who answered** and **Quality** in the Business review.
- **A table.** Tables over time are titled **Over time** and can be grouped by **Day**, **Week** or **Month**, with a **Change** column against the row before. Some reports have a choice of tables instead, such as **People** and **Teams** in Team performance.
- **View records**, which opens the matching records report with the same filters. For example, Business review leads to the Conversations log, and Response times & SLA to Escalations.

In a table:

- Click a column heading to sort by it. Click it again to reverse the order.
- The **Total** row at the bottom adds up counts and money. Rates are worked out from all the rows together, so a busy day weighs more than a quiet one. Median times in the Total row are the median for the whole period.
- When you group by week or month, a median time column reads **(median of days)**: a week's median first reply is the median of its days' medians.

**Open now** and **Overdue now** carry a **now** tag: they count tickets as they are right now, whatever the dates.

**WhatsApp spend is for the whole account.** Meta reports message spend for the whole WhatsApp account, not per number. When you narrow **Agents** to some of your agents, spend tiles carry a **Whole account** tag: the figure still covers every number. Cost per resolved conversation then has no value; clear the agents filter to see it. In Spend & usage, the agents filter only narrows the AI agent's figures.

### Read records

![The Tickets records report: a sortable table, one row per ticket](/screens/report-records.webp)

- The number of rows shows above the table, for example "809 tickets".
- Click a column heading to sort by it. Click it again to reverse the order.
- Times show as hours and minutes (1h 05m), and statuses and ratings as coloured labels.
- More than 50 rows come in pages. Choose **50 a page** or **100 a page**, and use the arrows to move between pages.
- Click a row with a ticket to open that ticket.

### Download a report

On a report's page, click **Download**. It always uses the filters shown.

- **CSV** opens in Excel or Google Sheets. A summary's CSV has its first table, one row per day.
- **PDF** (summaries only) opens a print-ready page with the tiles, charts and tables. In the print dialog, choose **Save as PDF**.
- **Email on a schedule…** starts a new scheduled email with this report and its filters filled in.

A records CSV holds up to 50,000 rows. When there are more, a line above the table says so: narrow the dates to get the rest.

### Download several at once

In the **Library**, click **Export…**. Pick the date, the agents and the reports, and choose a format:

- **CSV:** one file per report. Your browser may ask once to allow several downloads.
- **PDF:** one document with the chosen summaries. Records come as CSV only.

**Team** and **Assigned to** don't apply here. To narrow by them, download from the report's own page.

### Email reports on a schedule

<Steps>
<Step>
On **Reports**, open the **Scheduled emails** tab and click **New schedule**. Or, on a report's page, click **Download › Email on a schedule…**: that report and its filters are filled in for you.
</Step>
<Step>
Give it a **Name**, then choose **How often** and the hour under **At**.
</Step>
<Step>
Tick the **Reports to attach**, and under **Covering**, choose which agents and team the reports cover.
</Step>
<Step>
Under **Send to**, tick colleagues. Owners and admins can also add **Other email addresses** outside the workspace. A schedule can have up to 25 recipients.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save schedule**. The email has the headline numbers in the body and each report attached as a CSV.
</Step>
</Steps>

When it goes out:

- **Every day (yesterday)** sends the previous day's figures.
- **Every Monday (last 7 days)** covers the last full week, Monday to Sunday.
- **On the 1st (last month)** covers the previous month.
- Times follow your business hours' time zone.
- To see the same figures on screen, open a report with **Date: Yesterday**, **Last week (Mon–Sun)** or **Last month**.

Each schedule has a **Send now** button for a test. Switch it to **Paused** to stop it without deleting it. A workspace can have up to 20 schedules.

Schedules and links made before the reports were reorganised keep working. Day by day now opens the Business review, Team members and Teams open Team performance, and AI agents opens AI agent performance.

### What the report numbers mean

These are the definitions behind each tile's ⓘ.

| Number | What it counts |
| --- | --- |
| Conversations | Chats with a message from the customer, the AI or the team in these dates. |
| Handled by AI alone | Conversations the AI answered with nobody from the team writing, out of all conversations. **AI alone** is the same as a count. |
| With the team | Conversations someone from the team wrote in. |
| No reply | Conversations nobody answered: neither the AI nor the team. |
| Handovers | The AI handing a chat to a person, or someone taking it over. |
| Handover rate | Handovers (the AI handing over or someone taking over) out of conversations. |
| Satisfaction | Good ratings count fully and okay ones half, out of all ratings received. |
| Ratings received | How many customers answered the rating question. **Good**, **Okay** and **Bad** count each answer. |
| Tickets opened / Tickets resolved | Tickets opened, or resolved, in these dates. |
| Resolved on time | Resolved tickets that met their resolution target, out of all resolved tickets. |
| Median first reply | Half of tickets got their first reply from the team faster than this. |
| Median time to resolve | Half of tickets were resolved faster than this, from opened to resolved. |
| Escalations | Escalation levels reached; each level counts once per ticket. |
| Open now | Tickets open right now, whatever the dates. |
| Overdue now | Open tickets past a target right now, whatever the dates. |
| Spend | WhatsApp messages plus the Meta Business Agent, in these dates. |
| WhatsApp spend | What Meta charged for messages. Meta reports it for the whole WhatsApp account, not per agent. |
| Agent cost | What the Meta Business Agent's billed replies cost. |
| Cost per resolved conversation | WhatsApp and agent spend, divided by tickets resolved plus conversations the AI handled alone. |
| Budget used | This calendar month's spend against the monthly budget. |

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Reports are for supervisors, admins and owners** | Ask an admin to change your role in **Settings › Members**. |
| **Couldn't download** a report | Try again. If it happens again, give Helo.ai support the error reference. |
| **Only owners and admins send reports outside the workspace** | Remove outside addresses, or ask an admin to set up the schedule. |
| **Nobody to send it to: everyone on this schedule has left the workspace** | Edit the schedule and add someone. |
| Cost per resolved conversation is missing | Clear the agents filter so all agents are included. |
| A spend tile says **Whole account** | Meta reports message spend for the whole WhatsApp account. The figure covers every number, whatever the agents filter. |
| **Nothing in this period. Try a longer date range.** | Widen **Date**, or clear the other filters. |
| **The CSV has the first 50,000 of … rows** | Narrow the dates and download again for the rest. |
| Agent cost is empty | Add a payment method for the AI agent in Meta. |

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordions type="single">
<Accordion title="If the AI agent and my team both reply in a chat, where does it count?">
Under **With the team**. A chat only counts as **Handled by AI alone** when nobody from your team wrote in it. See [What the numbers mean](#what-the-numbers-mean).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Why doesn't spend match WhatsApp Manager exactly?">
Meta's billing figures arrive later and are counted per UTC day, while the rest of Analytics uses your business hours' time zone. Recent days can still change; **Settings › Billing** refreshes them every 6 hours.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Does total spend include the AI agent's cost?">
Yes. On the **Consumption** tab, **Total spend** adds message charges and the agent's cost together, and the **Meta Business Agent** figure shows the agent's part on its own.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I see the figures for one WhatsApp number?">
Yes, for conversations and tickets: each AI agent answers on its own number, so pick it under **Agents**. Spend is only reported for the whole account.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Why doesn't a report offer the Team or Assigned to filter?">
A report only shows the filters that change its numbers. Team and person narrow tickets, so reports about conversations, spend or the AI agent's usage have just **Date** and **Agents**.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What does (median of days) mean in a table?">
When you group a table by week or month, each row's median time is the median of its days. The **Total** row is the true median for the whole period.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="My old Day by day report or schedule is gone. What happened?">
Nothing is lost. Every summary now has its own table over time, grouped by **Day**, **Week** or **Month**. Old links and saved schedules open the report that replaced them: Day by day the Business review, Team members and Teams Team performance, and AI agents AI agent performance.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
