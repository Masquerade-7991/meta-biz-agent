Find the message you see to learn why it appears and what to do. Messages are shown here as the console words them.

## Before you start

If a message includes a **Reference** of 8 letters and numbers, keep it. An owner or admin can paste it into **Error reference** in **Settings › Activity** to see what happened, and Helo.ai support can use it too.

## Signing in and access

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="This link has expired or was already used.">
**Why:** sign-in, verification and password-reset links work once and for a limited time. Opening one twice, or opening an old email, fails.

**Do:** go back to the log-in screen and ask for a new link (**Resend link**, or **Email me a reset link**). Open the newest email.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Email or password is incorrect.">
**Do:** check that you're using the email you were invited with, and retype the password. If you've forgotten it, click **Forgot password?** on the log-in screen.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="This invite was revoked, has expired or was already answered.">
**Why:** an invite lasts 7 days, and an admin or owner may have revoked it.

**Do:** ask an admin or owner to send a new invite from **Settings › Members**.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Your role doesn't allow this. Ask an owner or admin.">
**Why:** each role can only do some things. Agents answer customers. Supervisors also create message templates and read reports. Admins run the workspace. Owners also manage WhatsApp accounts and billing.

**Do:** ask an owner or admin to do it, or to change your role in **Settings › Members**.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="A page or settings tab is missing">
**Why:** pages you can't use are hidden. **Analytics**, **Reports** and **Assets** need supervisor or above. **Activity** and **Billing** need admin or above. The **Account** tab in **Assets** is for owners only.

**Do:** ask an owner or admin if you need access.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Connecting a WhatsApp number

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="Only owners connect numbers.">
**Why:** connecting a number changes your WhatsApp Business account, so only owners can do it.

**Do:** ask a workspace owner to do it. See [Connect a WhatsApp number](/docs/guide/connect-number).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Needs attention (on a WhatsApp account)">
**Why:** one of the setup steps failed or ran out of time. The failed step is shown with a red mark and a reason underneath. The steps are: secure access to your account, connect your WhatsApp account to Helo.ai, register your number, bring over your chats and contacts, read your business details, and set up billing.

**Do:** as an owner, open **Assets › Account** and click **Retry the failed steps**. If a step says **Too late to retry**, connect the number again.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Waiting on you: add a payment method in WhatsApp Manager.">
**Why:** Meta bills your business for messages, and the number can't message customers until a payment method exists.

**Do:** click **Add a payment method**, add one in Meta, then come back and click **I've added it**.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="This WhatsApp account is already connected to another workspace.">
**Why:** an account can belong to only one workspace.

**Do:** disconnect it from the other workspace first, or use that workspace.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Too many signup attempts. Try again in an hour.">
**Do:** wait an hour, then connect again.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The signup didn't return a WhatsApp account and number. Please run it again.">
**Why:** the Meta window closed before the end, or a step in it wasn't finished.

**Do:** connect again and follow Meta's steps through to the final screen.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Connecting numbers from the console isn't switched on for this workspace yet. Talk to Helo.ai.">
**Do:** contact Helo.ai support. They can switch it on or finish the connection for you.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Meta refused the access token">
**Why:** the access that lets Helo.ai work with your WhatsApp account has expired or was withdrawn.

**Do:** tell your workspace owner. They can reconnect the account from **Assets › Account**, or ask Helo.ai support to replace the access.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="A number shows as disconnected, or messages stop arriving">
**Why:** the account may have been disconnected here, or removed on Meta's side. A number is also disconnected if it's moved off the platform.

**Do:** as an owner, open **Assets › Account** and check the account. If you disconnected it, connect it again. Otherwise, check the account in Meta's WhatsApp Manager.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Chats and replies

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="Reply window closed">
**Why:** WhatsApp only allows free-form replies for 24 hours after the customer's last message (the 24-hour reply window). Each new customer message restarts the 24 hours.

**Do:** send an approved template with **Send a template**. When the customer replies, you can chat freely again. A rating question can't be sent after the window has closed either.

<small>Source: [Meta — Send messages](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/messages/send-messages)</small>
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="WhatsApp only allows free replies within 24 hours of the customer's last message. Send a template instead.">
**Why:** the reply window has closed, as above.

**Do:** click **Send a template** in the chat.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="This chat is assigned to someone else.">
**Why:** another team member owns this chat's ticket, and agents can't take over someone else's work.

**Do:** ask them to hand it over, or ask a supervisor, admin or owner to reassign it. Agents can take unassigned chats.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="This chat already has an open ticket (#…). Continue there.">
**Why:** a chat has only one open ticket at a time.

**Do:** open the ticket named in the message and continue there.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The AI agent couldn't suggest a reply this time. Try again.">
**Do:** try again. If it happens again, give Helo.ai support the error reference.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Templates

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="Rejected, with a reason on the card">
**Why:** Meta reviewed the template and declined it. Common causes are mistakes in placeholders, a message that starts or ends with a placeholder, requests for sensitive details, or wording that breaks policy.

**Do:** edit the wording, give a realistic example for every placeholder and submit it again. See [Message templates](/docs/guide/templates).

<small>Source: [Meta — Template review](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/template-review)</small>
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Template … is paused; only approved templates can be sent.">
**Why:** Meta paused it because its quality dropped to low, usually after recipients blocked or reported it. A template that reaches low quality is paused for 3 hours the first time and 6 hours the second; the third time, it's disabled. Paused templates can't be sent, and the console only offers approved ones.

**Do:** wait for the pause to end, or unpause it in Meta's WhatsApp Manager. Review the wording and who you send it to, and send it only to people who want it.

<small>Source: [Meta — Template pausing](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/template-pausing)</small>
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Template names use lowercase letters, numbers and underscores only">
**Do:** rename it, for example `order_update`.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Add an example for {{1}} so Meta can review the template.">
**Do:** type a sample value for every placeholder in the examples area.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Supervisors, admins and owners create templates.">
**Do:** ask someone with that role. Only admins and owners can delete templates.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Messages that didn't go out

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="WhatsApp couldn't deliver a reply to this customer">
The chat shows this line, with the reason and an error number, when WhatsApp refused a message your team or the AI agent sent. Common reasons:

| Reason | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| It's more than 24 hours since the customer last wrote | Send a template instead. See [Message templates](/docs/guide/templates). |
| The customer turned off marketing messages from you | Don't send them marketing. They can turn it back on from your chat in WhatsApp, and utility messages can still reach them. |
| The number isn't on WhatsApp, or their app is too old | Check the number. If it's right, ask the customer to update WhatsApp. |
| This is a Meta test number | A test number can only message up to 5 verified recipients. Add the phone in the Meta app's **WhatsApp › API Setup › To** list, or use a real number. |
| Your WhatsApp account needs attention | Fix billing, policy or registration in WhatsApp Manager, then send again. |
| WhatsApp asked to slow down, or was unavailable | Wait a few minutes and send again. |

<small>Source: [Meta — Message status webhooks](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/webhooks/reference/messages/status)</small>
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Contacts and imports

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="Skipped rows after a CSV import">
After an import, the console says how many contacts were added, updated and skipped, and lists each skipped row as **Row N: reason**.

| Reason | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Looks like a local number. Add the country code, like +91 … | Put the country code in front of the number. |
| Phone must be 8 to 15 digits, country code first | Correct the number. |
| That email address doesn't look right | Fix or remove the email. |
| … must be a number / a date / one of: … | Match the contact field's type. |

Fix the rows in your file and import it again. Rows already added aren't duplicated.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Supervisors, admins and owners import contacts.">
**Do:** ask someone with that role. Only admins and owners change contact fields.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Reports and settings

<Accordions>
<Accordion title="Couldn't download … / The report couldn't be built. Please try again.">
**Do:** try again. If it keeps failing, give Helo.ai support the error reference.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Nobody to send it to: everyone on this schedule has left the workspace.">
**Do:** edit the scheduled report and add a recipient who's still in the workspace.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="A workspace needs at least one owner.">
**Do:** make someone else an owner first. Only owners can make or change owners.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="You can't remove yourself. / You're the only owner.">
**Do:** to leave, use **Leave this workspace** in **Settings › Profile**, or ask an admin or owner to remove you. If you're the only owner, make someone else an owner first.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Customers aren't getting an away message or rating question">
**Why:** on a number that Helo.ai manages for you, the console only listens and never messages customers by itself. Your team can still send replies themselves.

**Do:** nothing is wrong. On a number you connected yourself, check **Away message** and **Customer feedback** in **Settings › Hours & targets**.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Still stuck?

Contact Helo.ai support with the screen you were on, what you clicked, the exact message and any **Reference** shown.

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordions type="single">
<Accordion title="I'm an agent. How do I find out what went wrong?">
Copy the **Reference** from the message and send it to an owner or admin. They can look it up under **Error reference** in [Settings › Activity](/docs/guide/settings#activity-log).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Is there a record of who changed something?">
Yes. Owners and admins see every change, with who made it and the before and after, in [Settings › Activity](/docs/guide/settings#activity-log). Each ticket also keeps its own history.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="How do I know whether WhatsApp itself is down?">
Meta runs a status page for the WhatsApp Business Platform that reports outages and slowdowns. If it shows no known issues, the problem is more likely on your side or with the number. See [Meta — WhatsApp for Business Platform status](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/support/api-status-page).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="My message shows one tick for a long time. Did it fail?">
Not necessarily. One tick means WhatsApp accepted it; delivery is reported afterwards, and a failed message shows **WhatsApp couldn't deliver a reply to this customer**. See [Message statuses](/docs/guide/inbox#message-statuses).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Why can't I see a chat a colleague mentioned?">
Your workspace may let agents see only their own and unassigned chats. Ask a supervisor to reassign it to you, or see [Who sees which chats](/docs/guide/inbox#who-sees-which-chats).
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
