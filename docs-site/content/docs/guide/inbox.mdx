There is one chat per customer phone number. Chats appear when customers message your WhatsApp number. The AI agent answers them while it holds the chat.

## Taking over and handing back

- **Take over:** you answer instead of the AI. This opens a ticket if one isn't open already.
- **Hand back to the AI:** the AI answers again and the chat's ticket is resolved.
- The AI hands a chat to your team on its own when it's unsure, something seems wrong, or the customer asks for a person.

## Replying

Type a reply, or use a **canned response** for answers you send often. Canned responses are set up in **Settings › Canned responses**.

WhatsApp only lets you write freely within 24 hours of the customer's last message. After that, send an approved template.

## Teaching the agent from a chat

When the AI couldn't answer something, you can turn your answer into knowledge straight from the chat. Write the answer you'd want, and the agent uses it whenever a customer asks that.

## Customer details

The side panel shows the contact, their tags and fields, and the chat's ticket. You can block or unblock a customer on WhatsApp from there.

<Callout type="info">
Agents see the chats assigned to them or their teams, unless the workspace lets everyone see every chat. Supervisors and above always see every chat.
</Callout>
