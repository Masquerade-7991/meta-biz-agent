Set the agent's limits and its handover behaviour before real customers reach it.

![The Safety and handoff section](/screens/studio-safety.webp)

## Before you start

- Know who on your team will pick up the chats the agent hands over. See [Inbox](/docs/guide/inbox) and [Settings](/docs/guide/settings), where routing decides who gets each ticket.

## Steps

One **Save** button saves everything in this section.

<Steps>
<Step>
### Choose how it answers
Under **How your agent answers**, choose one:

- **Only from my knowledge:** the agent answers from what you've given it, and hands over when it doesn't know.
- **Can chat naturally:** the agent also makes small talk and gives general help within its role, still based on your knowledge.

For a business where wrong answers are costly, start with **Only from my knowledge**.
</Step>
<Step>
### Add words it never says
Type a word or phrase, then hit Enter to add it (up to 50 phrases, 60 characters each). Capital letters don't matter: "cheap" also covers "Cheap". The console may offer related forms, such as "Also add: …?", with **Add both** or **Dismiss**.

When the agent's reply would contain one of these phrases, Meta withholds that reply.
</Step>
<Step>
### Add topics it stays away from
Topics are broader than words: the agent politely declines anything about them. For example, "Competitors' prices". You can add up to 20 topics.
</Step>
<Step>
### Choose the handover message
Under **Handing over to your team**, pick **What it says when it hands over**:

- **Meta's standard message (in the customer's language)**.
- **Let the agent write it, to fit the chat**.
- **My own message**: write it yourself (up to 300 characters). A custom message can't be saved empty. It is sent exactly as written, in one language. If your agent speaks several languages, the console warns you that Meta's standard message matches the customer's language instead.
</Step>
<Step>
### Decide on follow-ups
Switch on **Check in when a customer goes quiet** to have the agent send one short message. Choose how long to wait in **After**, and whether to use Meta's standard message or **My own message** (up to 300 characters).

This follow-up is charged like any other message the agent sends.
</Step>
<Step>
### Save
Click **Save**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<small>Source: [Meta — Business Agent settings](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/meta-business-agent/reference/onboard/agent-settings)</small>

## When does the agent hand over?

The agent hands a chat over to your team when it is unsure, when something seems wrong, or when the customer asks for a person. Meta decides these triggers, and you can't switch them on or off. What you control is the message the customer sees.

To add your own rules, click **Add your own handoff rules**. It opens a new skill in [Abilities](/docs/guide/build-agent/abilities#skills), filled in with starting text that you can adapt. To change who gets the chats, click **Who receives handed-over chats**, which opens the support settings.

<small>Source: [Meta — Business Agent capabilities](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/meta-business-agent/capabilities)</small>

## What happens after a handover

The chat moves to your team in the Inbox, and a ticket opens for it. While your team holds the chat, the agent stays quiet. Anyone on your team who replies from the Inbox takes over the chat. When the issue is done, you can hand the chat back to the AI agent, which resolves the ticket. See [Inbox](/docs/guide/inbox) and [Tickets](/docs/guide/tickets).

## Always on

Whatever you choose, the agent never claims to be human when asked, never shares one customer's details with another, and never gives medical, legal or financial advice as fact.

## Good to know

- Longer follow-ups and longer answers cost more, because Meta bills agent messages by the amount of text.

## If something goes wrong

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Write the handoff message, or pick another option.** | You chose **My own message** but left it empty. Write the message, or pick another option. |
| **Write the follow-up message, or use Meta's standard one.** | The same, for the follow-up. |
| **You already have this word or phrase.** | The phrase is already in the list. |
| **Your changes weren't saved. Nothing has been lost.** | Click retry on the banner. |

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordions type="single">
<Accordion title="When does the agent hand a chat over?">
When it is unsure, when something seems wrong, or when the customer asks for a person. Meta decides these triggers; you can add your own rules as a skill with **Add your own handoff rules**. See [When does the agent hand over?](#when-does-the-agent-hand-over).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="What happens outside business hours?">
The agent keeps answering at all hours. A chat it hands over waits in the Inbox until your team picks it up. If you set an away message in [Settings](/docs/guide/settings), customers who write to your team outside business hours receive it.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I stop the agent talking about a topic?">
Yes. Add the topic under topics it stays away from, and the agent politely declines anything about it. For single words or phrases, add them to the words it never says.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I write my own handover message?">
Yes. Choose **My own message** and write up to 300 characters. It is sent exactly as written, in one language, so Meta's standard message is the better choice if your agent speaks several languages.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Does the agent answer while my team holds the chat?">
No. Once a chat is handed over or someone on your team replies, the agent stays quiet until you hand the chat back to the AI agent.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
