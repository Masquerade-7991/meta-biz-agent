Take the agent from private testing to answering your customers.

![The Publish section showing Who your agent answers and the Go live checklist](/screens/studio-publish.webp)

## Before you start

- A WhatsApp number connected to Helo.ai. The agent goes live on one specific number.
- A tested agent. See [Test your agent](/docs/guide/build-agent/test).
- For **Everyone**: a payment method on your WhatsApp account in Meta's Billing Hub.

## Steps

<Steps>
<Step>
### Add your test numbers
Under **Who your agent answers**, keep **Only my test numbers** selected (it's recommended while you try it). Enter a number with its country code, for example `+15551234567`, and press **Add**. Add yourself and colleagues.

You can add up to 20 numbers. Each one can chat with the agent as soon as it is added, even before it goes live. Remove a number with the x on its chip.
</Step>
<Step>
### Go through the checklist
The **Go live** card lists what's left: say what your agent does, give it knowledge, try it in Test & Eval, and choose who it answers. Each item opens the right section. A tick means done.
</Step>
<Step>
### Press Go live
A dialog asks "Ready to go live?" and reminds you that your agent becomes the main responder for the audience you chose, and that Meta charges for each message it sends. Press **Go live**. The status becomes **Testing** (test numbers only).
</Step>
<Step>
### Try it on a real phone
Message your number from a test number. Fix what feels off.
</Step>
<Step>
### Open it to everyone
Choose **Everyone**. Any customer who messages the number now reaches the agent. The status becomes **Live**.
</Step>
</Steps>

The recommended order is: add test numbers, keep **Only my test numbers**, switch on, test, then choose **Everyone**.

<small>Source: [Meta — Allowlist](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/meta-business-agent/reference/onboard/agent-allowlist) and [Meta — Get started](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/meta-business-agent/get-started)</small>

## Payment method for Everyone

Meta won't let the agent answer everyone until a payment method is added in Billing Hub. If you choose **Everyone** without one, Meta refuses and the console shows **Couldn't save to Meta** with Meta's reason. Add the payment method, then choose **Everyone** again. Answering only your test numbers works without one.

Meta charges per token for each agent message: $2.00 per million tokens from 1 August 2026, which is roughly 4 to 5 cents for a typical message. Messages the agent sends are billed even inside the 24-hour reply window.

<small>Source: [Meta — Business Agent settings](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/meta-business-agent/reference/onboard/agent-settings) and [Meta — Pricing for non-template messages](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/pricing/non-template-messages)</small>

## Pause and resume

Once the agent is on, the Go live card shows **Pause agent**.

- Pausing asks "Pause this agent?" Customers messaging the number then get no AI replies. Your team still sees their messages in the [Inbox](/docs/guide/inbox) and can answer them.
- The status becomes **Paused**. Press **Resume** to switch it on again. The toast says "Agent is live again".

When Meta's switch is turned off, the AI stops on all conversations. When it is turned back on, it applies to new conversations only.

<small>Source: [Meta — Business Agent settings](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/meta-business-agent/reference/onboard/agent-settings)</small>

## What customers experience

| Setting | A customer who messages the number |
| --- | --- |
| **Only my test numbers**, switched on | If their number is on your list, the agent replies. Everyone else gets no AI reply; your team handles those chats. |
| **Everyone**, switched on | The agent replies. It hands over to your team when it can't help. |
| **Paused** | No AI reply. Your team sees the message. |

## Good to know

- A phone number in the list must include the country code and be in the form `+` followed by digits. Otherwise: "This doesn't look like a valid number".
- You reach the limit at 20: "You've reached the limit of 20 numbers. Remove one to add another."
- Publishing is per number. Each WhatsApp number has its own agent.

## If something goes wrong

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Couldn't save to Meta** | Nothing changed. Read Meta's reason under the message. A missing payment method is the common one for Everyone. |
| **Couldn't switch your agent on. Nothing has changed.** | Press **Try again**. |
