Contacts keep what you know about your customers, so your team has context when it answers.

![The Contacts page with segments, tags and custom fields](/screens/contacts.webp)

Every customer who messages you becomes a contact. You can also add contacts yourself, or import them from a file.

## Before you start

- Everyone can view, add and edit contacts.
- Importing and deleting contacts, and managing segments, need a supervisor, admin or owner.

## Fields and tags

A contact has a **Name**, **Phone number** (with country code), **Email**, **Tags** and any **custom fields** you've added.

- **Tags** are free text, such as `vip` or `billing`. They're saved in lowercase, up to 30 per contact.
- **Custom fields** are extra details like city or renewal date. Add them under **Settings › Contact fields**, and choose text, number, date or a list of choices. Each field then shows as a column and in the contact's form.

Click **Add contact** to create one. Open a contact to edit it, jump to their chat with **Open chat**, or delete it.

## Import from a CSV file

Click **Import CSV** (supervisors and above). You can [download a small sample file](/samples/contacts-sample.csv) to see the layout.

![The import dialog showing how each column was matched](/screens/contacts-import.webp)

1. Click **Choose a CSV file**. The first row must hold the column headers.
2. Check how each column was matched. Unmatched columns show **Ignored**.
3. Optionally fill in **Tag everyone in this file** to add one tag to all of them, such as `diwali-2026`.
4. Click **Import N contacts** (the button shows the row count).

### Columns the import understands

| Contact detail | Header names that work |
| --- | --- |
| Phone (required) | Phone, Phone number, Mobile, Mobile number, WhatsApp, WhatsApp number, Number, Contact number |
| Name | Name, Full name, Customer name, Contact name |
| Email | Email, Email address |
| Tags | Tags, Labels (separate several with a comma, semicolon or vertical bar) |
| A custom field | The field's name |

Headers ignore capitals and punctuation. If no phone column is found, you see **No phone column found. Name a column "Phone", "Mobile" or "WhatsApp number".**

### Phone number rules

- Include the country code, with 8 to 15 digits in total. Spaces, dashes and brackets are fine.
- A 10-digit number with no `+` is treated as a local number and skipped. Write `+91 98765 43210`, not `98765 43210`.
- Numbers that already exist are updated, not duplicated. Existing tags are kept and new ones are added.
- A file can hold up to 10,000 rows.

### Skipped rows

When the import finishes you'll see "Added 40, updated 8, skipped 2", then each skipped row with its line number in your file and the reason, for example "Row 14: 9876543210 looks like a local number. Add the country code, like +91 9876543210." Line numbers match what you see in a spreadsheet, so you can fix those rows and import the file again. The list shows up to the first 100.

## Export

**Export CSV** downloads your contacts. Spreadsheet programs may treat text that starts with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` as a formula, so the export protects such cells by starting them with an apostrophe.

## Segments

A segment is a saved group that updates itself as contacts change. Use it to filter this page.

Click **New segment** and set:

- **Name**, for example "Active VIPs".
- **Has all these tags:** a contact must have every tag listed.
- **Last message from them**: **Any time**, **Within 7 days**, **Within 30 days** or **Within 90 days**.

To remove a segment, click **Delete segment**. Only the saved filter goes; the contacts stay.

## Permission to message people

WhatsApp's rules and Helo.ai's are the same: only message people who have agreed to hear from you.

- Get clear agreement before you message anyone, such as a tick box on your website or a form at your shop. The wording should say they're agreeing to hear from your business on WhatsApp, and name the business.
- Ask separately for different kinds of message (for example, order updates and offers), and tell people what they'll get.
- You can answer customers who message you first inside the 24-hour reply window. Starting a conversation, or writing after the window, needs an approved template.
- Respect every request to stop, however it arrives.

<small>Source: [Meta — Getting opt-in](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/getting-opt-in), [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/)</small>

### Opt-outs

When a customer replies **STOP** or **UNSUBSCRIBE**, the console marks the contact **Opted out**, so your team knows not to send them marketing. The chat still works, so you can answer if they write. If they reply **START**, the mark is removed. Customers can also pause marketing messages in WhatsApp's own settings, and the console picks that up too.

You can switch **Opted out of marketing messages** on or off in a contact's form. Only turn it off when the customer has asked to hear from you again.

To stop a customer messaging you at all, [block them](/docs/guide/inbox#block-a-customer).

<small>Source: [Meta — Marketing templates](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/templates/marketing-templates)</small>

## Delete a contact

Open the contact and click **Delete**. This also deletes their chat history and tickets, and can't be undone. If you only want to stop messaging them, use **Opted out of marketing messages** instead.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "isn't a phone number with a country code (8 to 15 digits)" | Fix the number in the file: add the country code and remove letters. |
| "looks like a local number" | Add the country code, such as `+91`. |
| "No phone column found" | Rename the phone column to **Phone**. |
| "Supervisors, admins and owners import contacts" | Ask one of them to import the file. |

## Frequently asked questions

<Accordions type="single">
<Accordion title="Does importing a file overwrite details we already have?">
Only where the file has a value. For a number that's already a contact, a filled-in name, email or field replaces the saved one, empty cells leave it as it is, and tags are added to the ones they have.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Do my column headers have to match exactly?">
No. Common names such as Mobile or WhatsApp number are recognised, and capitals and punctuation don't matter. Check the matches in the dialog before you import. See [Columns the import understands](#columns-the-import-understands).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I message everyone in a segment at once?">
No. Segments filter this page. Messages go out one chat at a time from the [Inbox](/docs/guide/inbox), and starting a conversation needs an approved template.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can a customer who opted out still write to us?">
Yes. Opting out only concerns marketing messages. They can still write to you, and you can answer them in the chat.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Does deleting a contact remove them from WhatsApp?">
No. It deletes the contact, their chat history and their tickets from Helo.ai only. If they write again, they appear as a new contact.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>
