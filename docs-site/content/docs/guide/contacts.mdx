title: Contacts
description: Your customers, their details, and groups to reach them.
---

Every customer who messages you becomes a contact. You can also add contacts yourself or import them.

## Fields and tags

Contacts have a name, a phone number, tags, and any **custom fields** you add in **Settings › Contact fields**.

## Import a CSV

Supervisors and above can import a CSV file:

1. Choose the file and match its columns to contact fields.
2. Optionally tag everyone in the file.
3. Review and import.

Rows that can't be imported are listed with their line number and the reason, so you can fix the file and try again. Phone numbers need their country code.

## Segments

A segment is a saved group, for example "has all these tags" or "last message from them in the past 30 days". Use segments to choose who gets a broadcast.

## Opting out

Contacts who opt out are never sent broadcasts, whatever segment they're in.
