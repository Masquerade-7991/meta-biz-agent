Settings has three groups: the workspace and its people, the rules of your support desk, and your own account. Which tabs you see depends on your role.

## Before you start

| Role | What it can change in Settings |
| --- | --- |
| Owner | Everything, including WhatsApp accounts, billing and other owners. |
| Admin | People (except owners), support rules, contact fields and shared canned responses. Can see Billing and Activity. |
| Supervisor | Their own profile. Support desk rules are changed by admins and owners. |
| Agent | Their own profile. |

If a link points to a tab your role can't open, you land on **Profile** instead.

## Workspace

| Tab | Who | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Members** | Everyone sees the list; admins and owners change it | Invite people, change roles, remove members. |
| **Activity** | Admins and owners | Who changed what and when. |
| **Billing** | Admins and owners see it; owners change the budget | What WhatsApp charged this month and a budget alert. |
| **WhatsApp** | Owners | Connected accounts and numbers, their setup steps and live events. |

### Members and roles

![Settings › Members with the invite form and the member list](/screens/settings-members.webp)

<Steps>
<Step>
Open **Settings › Members**.
</Step>
<Step>
Under **Invite people**, type the email address, pick a role and click **Send invite**.
</Step>
<Step>
The person gets an email with a link that works for 7 days. If they already use Helo.ai they keep their other workspaces and accept the invite inside the app.
</Step>
</Steps>

The four roles, from most to least access:

- **Owner:** everything, including WhatsApp accounts, billing and other owners.
- **Admin:** runs the workspace: people, AI agents, settings and message templates. Can't change owners.
- **Supervisor:** leads the team: sees every chat, reassigns tickets, creates message templates and reads reports.
- **Agent:** answers customers: chats, tickets and contacts.

Every workspace needs at least one owner. You can't remove yourself, and the only owner can't leave until someone else is made an owner. A person who leaves or is removed has their open work unassigned and is no longer given new tickets.

### Activity log

![Settings › Activity with filters and the list of recorded actions](/screens/settings-activity.webp)

The log records who did what in the workspace and what the system did on its own: ticket changes, AI agent edits, members, settings and more. It is kept for two years.

- Filter by **Person** (or **The system**), by **Area** and by a **From** and **To** date.
- Click **Download CSV** to save what the filters show.
- When someone reports an error with a reference, paste the 8 letters and numbers after "Reference:" into **Error reference** to find what happened. Routine plumbing such as messages and background jobs only shows when you look up a reference.

Each ticket also shows its own history.

### Billing

**Billing** shows what WhatsApp charged since the 1st of the month, from Meta's own figures, plus message counts by category and the AI agent's usage.

An optional **Monthly budget** emails the owners and notifies them at 80% and 100%. Messages keep sending; the budget only alerts you. Details of how Meta prices messages are under [Message templates](/docs/guide/templates) and [Analytics and reports](/docs/guide/analytics).

### WhatsApp

Owners see every connected account here: its numbers, whether it is **Connected** or **Needs attention**, how it is billed, and its setup steps. Use **Connect a number** to add one (see [Connect a WhatsApp number](/docs/guide/connect-number)).

- **Setup steps** show what happened when the number was connected, with a tick for each step that finished. If a step failed you get **Retry the failed steps**. If billing is waiting on you, add a payment method in Meta's WhatsApp Manager and click **I've added it**.
- **How this account maps to Helo.ai** shows how your Meta business, WhatsApp account and number are linked to your workspace.
- **Live events** shows how WhatsApp delivers customers' messages and delivery ticks to the console, and which other apps also receive them.
- **Disconnect** stops Helo.ai sending and receiving on that account's numbers. The account stays yours and you can connect it again.
- Accounts managed by Helo.ai show **Managed by Helo.ai**: the console reads them and configures the AI agent, but never changes their webhooks, registration, PIN or billing.

Each number's quality rating (High, Medium or Low), daily limit for new conversations and display name status appear on the **WhatsApp** page, with a **Check now** button. Quality drops when customers block or report you.

## Support desk

The support desk settings are changed by admins and owners.

### Hours and response targets

![Settings › Hours & response targets](/screens/settings-hours.webp)

- **Business hours:** the time zone and the opening hours for each day. Response-time targets only count while you are open.
- **Away message:** sent to customers outside hours. Leave it empty to send nothing.
- **Response targets:** for each priority (Urgent, High, Normal, Low), how long a ticket can wait for its first reply and for resolution, in business-time minutes. Resolution must be at least as long as first reply. Tickets show a countdown and the bell warns before time runs out.
- **Default routing:** who gets a new ticket when no routing rule decides. People set to away or offline are skipped.
- **Customer feedback:** after a ticket is resolved, ask the customer how it went with three WhatsApp buttons (Good, Okay, Bad). You can change the question.
- **Who sees which chats:** supervisors, admins and owners always see every chat. You can limit agents to their own.

On a number the console only listens to (one managed by Helo.ai), the away message and feedback question are not sent.

### Teams and people

Create a team for each kind of work, such as Billing or Orders, with a team lead. Under **People**, set each person's:

- **Status:** online, away or offline. Only available people get new tickets.
- **Open limit:** the number of open tickets after which they get no more (1 to 500, or empty for no limit).
- **Skills:** short labels such as `hindi`, so a rule can pick a Hindi speaker.

A team can't be deleted while a routing rule sends tickets to it. Change the rule first.

### Routing rules

![Settings › Routing rules](/screens/settings-routing.webp)

When a ticket opens, the rules are checked from the top. **The first rule that matches decides; the rest are skipped.** If none matches, the default routing applies. Someone already looking after the chat keeps it.

- A rule has conditions (for example when the customer writes, why the ticket opened, contact tags, whether the team is working) and actions (for example assign to a team, a person or a skill, add tags).
- A rule with no conditions catches every ticket that reaches it, so keep specific rules above general ones. Use the arrows to reorder.
- Only available people under their open limit are given tickets.
- **Try it** shows which rule a new ticket would meet, using the saved rules.

### Escalation

![Settings › Escalation matrix](/screens/settings-escalation.webp)

As a ticket uses up its response targets it moves up one **level** at a time, up to three levels per priority. Levels fire in order and only once each per ticket, after the level before.

For each level you choose:

- the clock (first reply or resolution) and the percent of the target at which it fires (100% is the breach);
- who is alerted: the assignee, the team lead, supervisors, or admins and owners;
- whether to **Also email them** as well as the bell;
- optionally move the ticket to another team or **Raise priority**.

Open tickets are checked every minute, and times count business hours. If you turn escalation off, tickets still show as overdue but nobody is alerted. A level must do something: alert someone, move the ticket or raise its priority.

### Canned responses

Saved replies that your team inserts in the Inbox by typing the shortcut, such as `/refund`. Shortcuts use letters, numbers, `-` and `_`. A response can also change the ticket after it is sent: wait on the customer, resolve it, or add tags. Admins and owners manage the shared ones.

### Contact fields

Extra fields on every contact, such as City. Choose a name and a type (text, number, date or a choice list). Only admins and owners change them.

## Profile

**Profile** is the same for everyone:

- **Your name:** shown to your workspace and in invite emails.
- **Email:** to change it, you enter your current password and we send a confirmation link to the new address.
- **Password:** at least 8 characters. Changing it logs you out on every other device.

Switch light and dark mode with the toggle in the top bar. The workspace switcher at the top of the sidebar moves you between workspaces if you belong to several.

## API tokens

**API tokens** let a script, or the **Try in Playground** button in the [API reference](/docs/developers/api), use the console's API as you. Everyone can create their own.

- A token works in the workspace you're in when you create it, with **your current role**. If your role changes, the token's does too. If you leave the workspace or are removed, it stops working.
- Give it a name you'll recognise, such as *Docs try it*, and choose when it expires: 30 days, 90 days (the default), 1 year or never.
- **Copy the token straight away.** It's shown once. Afterwards the list shows only its first characters (`hk_…`), when it was last used and when it expires.
- **Revoke** a token you no longer need, or one that might have leaked. Anything using it stops working at once.
- Owners and admins also see **All tokens in this workspace** and can revoke anyone's.

A token can read your account but can't change it: no password, email, workspace or token changes. Do those in the console.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Your role doesn't allow this. Ask an owner or admin.** | Ask an admin or owner to make the change or raise your role. |
| **This person is already in this workspace** or **already invited** | Resend the invite from the Members list instead of creating another. |
| **A workspace needs at least one owner** | Make someone else an owner before demoting or removing the last one. |
| **This invite was revoked, has expired or was already answered** | Ask an admin to send a new one. |
| **Invalid API token** | The token is wrong, revoked or expired, or its owner left the workspace. Create a new one under **API tokens**. |
| **Check the hours for …: closing must be after opening** | Fix the times for that day. |
| **Check the … targets: both above zero, and resolution at least as long as first response** | Raise the resolution time or lower the first reply time. |
| **The rule … sends tickets to this team. Change the rule first.** | Edit the routing rule, then delete the team. |
| **… is already used by …** (canned response) | Pick a different shortcut. |

More fixes are in [Troubleshooting](/docs/guide/troubleshooting).
