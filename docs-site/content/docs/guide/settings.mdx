## Workspace

| Setting | Who | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Members** | Admins and owners | Invite people, change roles, remove members. |
| **Activity** | Admins and owners | Who changed what and when, kept for 2 years. Filter it, or download a CSV. |
| **Billing** | Owners | How you pay for WhatsApp messages. |
| **WhatsApp** | Owners | Connected accounts and numbers, their setup steps, how they map, and the number's PIN. |

## Support desk

| Setting | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Hours & response targets** | Business hours, targets for first reply and resolving, and the satisfaction question. |
| **Teams & people** | Teams, each person's skills, how many tickets they can hold, and whether they're available. |
| **Routing rules** | Who gets a new ticket. The first matching rule wins. Otherwise the default routing applies. Only available people under their limit get tickets. |
| **Escalation** | Who is alerted, in order, when a ticket's target is at risk. Alerts go to the bell and optionally by email. |
| **Canned responses** | Saved replies for the Inbox. |
| **Contact fields** | Custom fields on contacts. |

## You

**Profile:** your name, email and password. Switch light or dark mode with the toggle in the top bar.
