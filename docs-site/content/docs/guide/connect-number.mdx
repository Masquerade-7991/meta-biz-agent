---
title: Connect a WhatsApp number
description: Bring your number into Helo.ai through Meta's own signup.
---

Connecting a number is done by the workspace **owner**. Click **Connect a number** on Home, on the WhatsApp page, or in **Settings › WhatsApp**.

## What happens

<Steps>
<Step>

**Meta's signup opens.** You sign in to Facebook and go through Meta's own WhatsApp signup. It's Meta's page, not ours, so your login stays with Meta.

</Step>
<Step>

**You pick or create the assets.** That means your business portfolio, a WhatsApp Business account, and the phone number. You can use a brand-new number, or a number you already use in the WhatsApp Business app. That app keeps working.

</Step>
<Step>

**You grant permissions.** This lets Helo.ai manage messaging for that account on your behalf.

</Step>
<Step>

**Helo.ai finishes the setup.** It registers the number, subscribes it to messages, and reads its details. Each step shows as done, waiting on you, or failed with a **Retry** button.

</Step>
</Steps>

## How your assets map into Helo.ai

| From Meta | Lands in Helo.ai as |
| --- | --- |
| Business portfolio | The business behind your workspace |
| WhatsApp Business account | The account your workspace sends and receives through |
| Phone number | The number your AI agent and team answer on |
| Permissions you grant | What Helo.ai may do for that account, and nothing more |

The console shows this map before you start, then again afterwards with the details Meta reported.

## Billing

Meta charges for WhatsApp conversations. Either Helo.ai bills you, or you pay Meta directly. If you pay Meta directly, the billing step shows **Waiting on you**. Add a payment method in WhatsApp Manager, then click **I've added it**.

## Your PIN

Registering a number sets a six-digit PIN for it. Owners can see it in **Settings › WhatsApp**. Keep it safe. Meta asks for it if you ever move the number.

<Callout type="info">
Some setups open Meta's signup in the same tab. When you finish there, Helo.ai completes the connection on its side, and the number appears in your workspace.
</Callout>
